#### EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR'S ACTIVITY STATEMENT {.text-center}

Nick Silvestri presented an overview of the World Cube Association's activities during the 2024 calendar year.

2,715 competitions took place in 2024, over two and and a half times as many as 2022 (1,079). 64 thousand competitors attended these competitions, 51% were new competitors. Competitions were sanctioned in 98 countries. Continental Championships were held in six continents, supported by the WCA's partner, Rubik's. 

At the end of 2024 the WCA recognized 45 Regional Organizations, including 9 added during the year. There were 538 WCA Volunteers, plus 114 Trainee Delegates. The WCA Integrity Committee and WCA Major Championship Team were founded during the year.

Future key projects would include a website redesign, support for developing regions, training systems for Delegates, and improvements for spectator experience. 

Following massive expansion in the scope and operations of the WCA in recent years, an updated strategy had been developed. The WCA's new Purpose Statement is: To empower the global speedcubing community and uphold a fun and fair competitive environment for all. The WCA's Values are: Community, Fairness, Fun, Excellence, and Volunteerism. The WCA's Board's updated Strategic Plan also included five key results areas: Growth, Systems, Partnerships, Events & Championships, and Volunteers.
 
#### FINANCIAL REPORT FOR 2024 {.text-center}

Raymond Goslow presented the World Cube Association 2024 Financial Report, as included in the meeting materials.

Revenue had increased by 12.5% relative to 2023. The main revenue sources were sponsorships ($231k) and dues ($462k), with other revenue sources representing less than 5% of total revenue.

Overall expenses nearly doubled year-on-year, however this new level of spending was largely a process of catching up with revenue levels in the previous years, resulting in continued overall growth of the WCA’s assets. 

The percentage of the WCA's assets held in cash decreased below 50%, however cash on hand was still sufficient to maintain regular operations for 6 months. The overall financial position of the WCA remained stable and strong. Other significant assets included the WCA trademark and the value of software that the WCA has spent money to develop and capitalized as an asset.

The WCA funds a variety of programs to further its mission; total net expenditure on program
activities increased by 137% relative to 2023 due to growth in a number of initiatives. Major championship competition support, which includes direct payments to championship organization teams and spending on the WCA’s presence at championships was the largest expense in this category. The WCA also provides travel funding, equipment funding, and Regional Organization support funding.

Supporting expenses, including accounting, legal, insurance, web hosting, and software contractors increased year-on-year, however supporting expenses declined from 40% to 30% of overall expenses, meaning a larger proportion of the WCA’s expenses were directed toward program spending.

There being no questions the chair called a vote to accept the report.

In favour: 182

Opposed: 0

#### BYLAWS AMENDMENT - MEMBER REMOVAL {.text-center}

Kerrie Jarman explained the proposed amendment. Under the existing Bylaws, a Regional Organization could only be removed by a full member vote. The amendment would permit the WCA Board to remove Regional Organizations, subject to a two thirds voting majority.

There being no questions the chair called a vote to approve the Amendment.

Approve the Amendment to the Bylaw 2.9 - Resignation and Termination:

In favour: 161

Opposed: 12

Abstain: 5
